About MITA High School

Our History & Foundation

MITA High School Ssanga was established by four visionary founders with a commitment to provide quality secondary education in the Wakiso District. The school was strategically named "MITA" in honor of these four founding fathers, representing their collective vision for educational excellence.

School Highlights

Mita High School Ssanga is a secondary school offering both Arts and Sciences streams, marked as one of the best secondary schools in the Wakiso District. The school operates as both a Day and Boarding institution, welcoming students of all races and backgrounds.

🎯 Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in education, producing graduates who are academically proficient, morally upright, and equipped to contribute meaningfully to society.

Academic Programs

Science Track: Preparing students for careers in medicine, engineering, agriculture, and technology.

Arts Track: Developing skills in humanities, languages, social sciences, and business studies.

Vocational Training (DIT): Offering practical skills in Electricity, Tailoring, and various technical disciplines.
